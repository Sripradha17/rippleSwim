Lane 1

Water Babies

6 months-3 years | Parent + child | 30 minutes

Gentle water play, body position basics, and trust-building for the earliest swimmers.

Lane 2

Beginners

4-6 years | Small group | 35 minutes

Floating, kicking, breath control, and confidence around the wall and shallow water.

Lane 3

Intermediate

7-12 years | Small group | 45 minutes

Stroke foundations, safe deep-water skills, and smoother movement across the pool.

Lane 5

Adult Lessons

18+ | Private or paired | 45 minutes

Supportive coaching for beginners, returning swimmers, and adults refining technique.
