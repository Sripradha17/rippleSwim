Classes and levels

Find the right lane for every swimmer.

Each lane is built around age, comfort level, and the pace needed for strong fundamentals in and around the water.

Lane 1

Water Babies

Ages: 6 months-3 years | Format: Parent + child | Length: 30 minutes

This lane introduces babies and toddlers to the water through songs, supported floats, gentle splashing, and comfort-building routines that help both child and caregiver feel at ease.

Lane 2

Beginners

Ages: 4-6 years | Format: Small group | Length: 35 minutes

Beginner swimmers learn water entry, floating, kicking, breath control, and safe wall returns in a supportive setting that keeps early lessons encouraging and structured.

Lane 3

Intermediate

Ages: 7-12 years | Format: Small group | Length: 45 minutes

Intermediate swimmers connect breathing, kicking, and arm patterns into smoother freestyle and backstroke foundations while growing confidence in deeper water.

Lane 4

Advanced / Stroke Refinement

Ages: 10+ | Format: Small group or semi-private | Length: 50 minutes

This lane focuses on stronger technique, endurance, starts and turns basics, and refining stroke mechanics for swimmers who are already water-safe and ready for precision.

Lane 5

Adult Lessons

Ages: 18+ | Format: Private or paired | Length: 45 minutes

Adults receive calm, individualized coaching whether they are learning from the beginning, rebuilding comfort in the water, or refining technique for lap swimming.

We can recommend the right lane based on age, comfort, and goals.
